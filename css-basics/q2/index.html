Welcome to Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan, India. It is famous for its beautiful palaces, colorful markets, and rich traditions. It is also known as the Pink City.

History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. The city is known for its planned streets, historical monuments, and royal architecture.

Famous Places

Jaipur has many popular tourist attractions, including Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace, and Jantar Mantar. These places attract visitors from around the world.

Culture and Food

Jaipur is famous for traditional Rajasthani clothing, folk music, handicrafts, and delicious food such as Dal Baati Churma and Ghewar.
